Division 05

Printing

Robotic screen printing, digital DTG, and rotary printing — vivid, durable designs delivered with precision at any production volume.

Overview

Every Design. Any Volume.

Our printing division is equipped with advanced robotic automatic screen printing carousels, digital direct-to-garment machines, and rotary flat-bed printers — giving us the flexibility to handle everything from simple one-colour logos to complex all-over photographic prints.

Colour matching is done in-house using a spectrophotometer — ensuring every Pantone shade hits accurately across the full production run.

Key Specifications
Print Methods: Screen, Digital DTG, Rotary, Sublimation
Colour Range: Up to 12 colours per design
Daily Capacity: 30,000 pieces/day (screen)
Special Finishes: Puff, Glitter, Foil, Discharge, Embroidery
Colour Matching: Spectrophotometer, Pantone library
Ink Standards: OEKO-TEX & REACH compliant inks
What We Print

From branded chest logos and all-over prints to heat transfers and embroidery combinations — our printing division handles the full spectrum of decorative techniques used in today's fashion and sportswear market.

All inks and materials are REACH and OEKO-TEX compliant, ensuring the finished garment is safe for end consumers and meets import regulations in the EU and North America.
