Stitching

Expert garment assembly lines running single needle, overlock, flatlock, and cover-stitch operations — with AQL inspection at every stage.

Overview

Where Garments Come Together

Our stitching floor is the heart of Champion Sprout's garment manufacturing operation. Multiple assembly lines — each configured for specific garment types — run with inline QC checkpoints every 20 minutes to catch defects before they multiply.

Managed by GM Bilal Usman, the stitching division produces T-shirts, sweatshirts, polo shirts, hoodies, trousers, shorts, and jackets — handling both basic and fashion-forward constructions with equal precision.

Our Quality Promise

Every garment passes through a minimum of four quality checkpoints — seam inspection, measurement check, appearance audit, and final AQL sampling — before it enters the finishing department. Our defect rate is maintained below 1.5%.

We also offer value-added services such as label attachment, hangtag application, folding, and polybag packing in buyer-specified configurations — ready to ship directly to retail.
