Division 04

Cutting

CAD/CAM precision cutting systems that minimise fabric waste, maximise yield, and ensure every panel is cut to exact pattern specifications.

Overview

Precision from the First Cut

Our cutting department operates computer-aided spreading and cutting systems, generating optimised lay plans that maximise marker efficiency and reduce fabric waste. Straight knife, band knife, and die-cutting machines handle everything from basic shapes to complex pattern pieces.

Each cut bundle is tagged, counted, and matched to its stitching line — a zero-loss handoff system that eliminates mismatched panels and production delays.

Key Specifications
System: CAD/CAM Spreading & Cutting
Machine Types: Straight Knife, Band Knife, Die Cut
Daily Capacity: 25,000 pieces/day
Lay Length: Up to 30 metres per lay
Marker Efficiency: 85–92% fabric utilisation
Bundle Tracking: Barcode tagging per bundle
Zero-Waste Approach

Our CAD system generates optimised markers that push fabric utilisation beyond 90%, significantly reducing waste compared to manual cutting. Fabric offcuts are collected, sorted, and recycled into industrial materials.

Every cut bundle is tracked via barcode from the cutting table to the stitching line, giving production managers real-time visibility into WIP status.
