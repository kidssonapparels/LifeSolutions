Spinning

High-quality yarn production with state-of-the-art ring spinning technology — consistent fineness, strength, and purity from Ne 20 to Ne 60.

Overview

Precision Yarn at Every Count

Our spinning division operates with advanced ring-spinning and open-end spinning machinery, producing high-tenacity yarn for diverse knitted and woven applications. Every batch undergoes rigorous count verification, strength testing, and evenness measurement.

We handle 100% cotton, polyester, cotton-poly blends, and viscose — delivering consistent quality even at high production volumes.

Key Specifications
Yarn Count Range: Ne 20 to Ne 60
Fibre Types: Cotton, Polyester, Viscose, Blends
Spinning System: Ring Spinning & Open End

Quality Tests: Uster, Count, Strength, Evenness
Packaging: Cone, Cheese, Hank
Why Our Spinning

Our spinning unit feeds directly into our knitting division, ensuring complete control of yarn quality from the first stage of production. This vertical integration eliminates supplier dependency and guarantees consistency across every batch.

With in-house lab testing and real-time production monitoring, we deliver yarn that meets the strictest international specifications every time.
